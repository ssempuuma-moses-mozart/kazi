Meet the Team

    About

    Kazi Valuers & Surveyors is a professional real estate valuation and property advisory firm led by a RICS-qualified Chartered Valuer, duly registered with the Surveyors Registration Board (SRB) of Uganda. We deliver independent, objective, and market-driven real estate valuation and advisory services in full compliance with internationally recognized standards, including the International Valuation Standards (IVS) and the RICS Valuation - Global Standards ("Red Book"), as well as all applicable Ugandan statutory and regulatory requirements. Our expertise extends to real estate feasibility studies and property management, covering a diverse range of asset classes such as residential, commercial, industrial, retail, hospitality, and agricultural properties. We serve a broad clientele that includes private individuals, corporate organizations, financial institutions, development partners, and public sector entities, supporting informed decision-making in investment, financing, and dispute resolution. All assignments are executed with strict adherence to professional ethics, independence, and rigorous quality control standards.

    Our Service Lines

    Delivered with independence, technical rigor, and a commitment to international standards.

    Real Estate Valuation

    Kazi delivers independent, comprehensive property valuation and advisory services tailored to meet the specific needs of every client. Our valuation expertise spans a wide range of asset classes, including residential, commercial, industrial, retail, hospitality, and agricultural properties. Each assignment is conducted using robust, market-based methodologies and supported by in-depth research and analysis to ensure accuracy and reliability. Our team applies internationally recognized standards, including the International Valuation Standards (IVS) and the RICS Valuation - Global Standards ("Red Book"), alongside relevant Ugandan regulatory requirements. This ensures that our reports are not only compliant but also credible and defensible. With our technical expertise, local market knowledge, and a commitment to independence, Kazi provides clients with clear, well-reasoned insights that support confident decision-making.

    Agency Services

    We provide professional agency services across residential, commercial, and retail property sectors, delivering tailored solutions that align with our clients' objectives. Acting in our clients' best interests, we ensure a seamless and transparent transaction process from initial engagement through to completion. Our services include strategic property marketing and targeted exposure, buyer and tenant sourcing, skilled negotiation support, and coordination of due diligence processes. We also offer continuous advisory support throughout the transaction lifecycle, ensuring informed decision-making at every stage.

    Market Research and Advisory

    We provide market research and advisory services which support informed real estate investment and development decisions. Our insights are grounded in rigorous analysis, local market knowledge, and a clear understanding of evolving industry trends. Our services include market feasibility studies, highest and best use analysis and demand and supply assessments. Through comprehensive research and data-driven insights, we help clients understand market dynamics, demand drivers, absorption trends, and competitive positioning enabling well-informed, strategic decision-making in the property sector.

    Property Management

    We deliver comprehensive property management services for residential, retail, and commercial assets, ensuring efficient operations and sustained asset performance. Our approach is proactive and client-focused, designed to optimize income, maintain property condition, and enhance long-term value. Our services include rent collection and administration, tenant liaison and relationship management, lease administration and renewals, and coordination of maintenance and repairs. We also provide ongoing asset performance monitoring and reporting to support informed decision-making. Through a structured and professional management approach, we aim to preserve asset value, maximize returns, and ensure the smooth day-to-day operation of our clients' properties.

    Our Work Approach

    Delivering excellence through a structured and professional process.

    1
    Client Consultation

    We start by engaging with our clients to fully understand their objectives, scope of work, property specifics, and the purpose of the valuation. This ensures clarity and alignment from the very beginning.

    2
    Property Inspection

    A thorough on-site inspection is conducted to assess the property's location, condition, improvements, accessibility, and the surrounding environment, providing a solid foundation for accurate valuation.

    3
    Market Research

    We collect and analyze relevant market data, including recent transactions, comparable properties, rental trends, economic indicators, and sector-specific performance metrics, to inform our assessment.

    4
    Data Analysis

    Leveraging established valuation methodologies, we evaluate all gathered information to determine the most accurate and defensible market value of the property.

    5
    Valuation Reporting

    A comprehensive and professionally prepared report is delivered, detailing assumptions, methodology, analysis, and our final opinion of value, ensuring transparency and reliability for our clients.
